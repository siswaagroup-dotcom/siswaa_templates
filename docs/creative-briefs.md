# Pets Creative Briefs

Pets 01 has its authoritative standalone specification in [`src/templates/pets/01-curious-dog/meta.ts`](../src/templates/pets/01-curious-dog/meta.ts). The table below preserves the five earlier Pets concept directions; variants 02-05 still use the shared legacy renderer and have not yet been converted to standalone packages or passed the strict per-template gates. Earlier registry metadata is in [`src/templates/pets/design.meta.js`](../src/templates/pets/design.meta.js).

| Design | Big idea and metaphor | Signature moment | Memory hook | Motion / voice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Curious Dog | A pet's curiosity becomes the clinic's welcome. | An illustrated dog tracks the pointer and tilts when clicked. | An oversized warm portrait looks back at the visitor. | Playful and gently reassuring. |
| Little Wild Ones | Care is a trail with clear, manageable stops. | Tap the three-stop route to change the selected destination and note. | A pawprint path laid over countryside photography. | Organic, adventurous, practical. |
| The Good Life | Care is the comfort of home, told like a quiet journal. | A keyboard-operable slider reveals two companion moments. | Dark editorial photography and a tactile image reveal. | Cinematic, intimate, unhurried. |
| Paws & Play | Everyday pet play becomes a bright curated shop. | Add an item to the local demo bag; checkout is explicitly not real. | Citrus stage, oversized WOOF burst, and playful product shelf. | Energetic, witty, inclusive. |
| Whiskerwell Clinic | A clinic visit becomes an understandable patient journey. | Patient-guide tabs update the current step and explanation. | Teal appointment panel and clear dashboard composition. | Precise, steady, supportive. |

The standalone 01 experience has its own navigation, hero, section order, footer, palette, and responsive composition. Do not infer that variants 02-05 pass the new folder, image, animation, or visual-proof checks from their earlier concept descriptions.

## Scope boundary

Pets 02-05 and the remaining ten industries still need standalone packages, their own image manifests and animation proofs. The current automated global check reports these missing packages as failures rather than claiming a uniqueness result for generated legacy records.
